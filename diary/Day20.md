# Day 20 – Final Project Evaluation

**Date:** 27 July 2026

## Objective

The objective of the day was to undergo the final evaluation of the machine learning project developed during the four-week training programme.

---

## Session Summary

The final day of the four-week training programme was dedicated to project evaluation. During the session, my project, **HealthGuardian AI: Intelligent Diabetes Risk Assessment & Personalized Health Recommendation System**, was evaluated.

The evaluation focused on the project work completed during the training period and the practical application of the machine learning concepts covered throughout the programme.

This session marked the completion of the training programme and provided an opportunity to assess the work carried out during the project development process.

---

## Learning Outcome

The final evaluation helped me reflect on the technical knowledge and practical skills gained throughout the training. Working on HealthGuardian AI strengthened my understanding of data preprocessing, machine learning model development, model evaluation, and application implementation.

The overall training experience enhanced my confidence in applying theoretical concepts to practical machine learning projects and provided valuable exposure to the complete project development process.
